# Living Memory — Video Submission Script & Production Guide

> **Target**: ~3 minutes | **Tone**: Warm, hopeful, startup-level polish  
> **Purpose**: Google Gemini 3 Hackathon submission — hook judges, showcase every feature

---

## 🎬 PART 1: HOOK & POSITIONING (0:00–0:35)

### [0:00–0:10] COLD OPEN

**VISUAL**: Start on a vintage family photo (use `pic1.PNG` or similar from `/public`). Grainy, warm, nostalgic.

**SPEAK**:
> "Every family has a shoebox. Photos that fade. Stories that get lost. But what if those stories could live forever?"

**EDIT**: Slow zoom on the photo. Ambient sound (optional: soft vinyl crackle). Cut to black or Living Memory logo.

---

### [0:10–0:35] THE PROBLEM + POSITIONING

**VISUAL**: Quick montage — old albums, faded prints, phone in hand scrolling. Then: Living Memory app logo / landing page.

**SPEAK**:
> "Remento proved the need — people want to preserve family stories. Living Memory is the AI-native version: voice, video, scale. We use Gemini 3 to turn scattered photos into narrated memory films you can actually share."

**EDIT**: B-roll of families, elders, nostalgic moments. Keep pace brisk. End on app open.

---

## 🎯 PART 2: EULOGY & EVA (0:35–1:10)

### [0:35–0:55] EULOGY INSPIRATION

**VISUAL**: Show the intro page — EVA orb, aurora glow, vintage photos in background (`pic1`–`pic9`).

**SPEAK**:
> "We were inspired by the idea of a companion that remembers — like the Eulogy concept. Not morbid. Hopeful. Someone who listens, asks the questions you've always wanted to ask, and keeps your stories safe."

**EDIT**: Let EVA intro play — show "Hello, I'm Eva. I keep your memories safe... forever." and 1–2 more lines. Synced typewriter + voice = powerful.

---

### [0:55–1:10] EVA AS THE HOOK

**VISUAL**: EVA orb on album page. Click to open capture modal.

**SPEAK**:
> "This is EVA — our AI companion. She's powered by Gemini Live. You don't just upload photos. You talk to her. Show her. Tell her the story. She remembers every word."

**EDIT**: Show orb hover/pulse. Open capture modal. Glimpse of split layout (camera + chat).

---

## 📸 PART 3: CAPTURE TYPES & HOOKS (1:10–1:50)

### [1:10–1:30] CAPTURE OPTIONS

**VISUAL**: Capture modal — camera feed, scan button, gallery picker, microphone.

**SPEAK**:
> "There are three ways to add memories. Point your camera at a physical photo — we detect and extract it. Or upload from your gallery. Or scan an old print in a frame. Either way, you're not just adding a file. You're starting a conversation."

**EDIT**: Demo each:
1. **Camera capture**: Show live feed, point at photo, click scan.
2. **Gallery**: Click gallery, pick photo, upload.
3. **Scan**: If you have a physical photo, show the 4-corner detection / Nano Banana style extraction.

**CUE**: Highlight the "Nano Banana" / Gemini Vision crop — we extract the photo from the frame, even from a messy scan.

---

### [1:30–1:50] THE CONVERSATION HOOK

**VISUAL**: EVA asking questions. User (or voiceover) responding. Aurora wave reacting to speech.

**SPEAK**:
> "Once you've captured a photo, EVA asks. Who's in this? What happened? Why did it matter? You talk. She listens. She extracts the facts — who, what, when, where, why — and builds a story. No forms. No questionnaires. Just a conversation."

**EDIT**: Show transcript, fact extraction UI, completeness bars if visible. Emphasize: *conversation*, not form-filling.

---

## 📚 PART 4: REMENTO-STYLE STORY (1:50–2:15)

### [1:50–2:05] STORY WEAVING

**VISUAL**: Album editor — media pool, timeline, photos in order.

**SPEAK**:
> "Remento made books. We make films. You drag photos into the order you want. The AI generates narration that fits — opening, middle, closing. It knows the whole album. It fills in gaps. It tells one coherent story."

**EDIT**: Show drag-drop reorder. Narration tab. Editable segments. Maybe a quick "Generate Narration" click.

---

### [2:05–2:15] MULTI-PERSPECTIVE (BONUS)

**VISUAL**: If you have multi-contributor UI or mention of it — show it. Otherwise, voiceover only.

**SPEAK**:
> "Everyone sees things differently. Mom remembers the laughter. Dad remembers the drive. We're built to collect multiple perspectives and weave them into one story."

**EDIT**: Brief. Sets up future. Skip if time is tight.

---

## 🎬 PART 5: ANIMATION TYPES TO EDIT (2:15–2:45)

### [2:15–2:30] VEO 3 & GROK

**VISUAL**: Album editor — inspector open, "Bring to Life" or animation buttons. Play a VEO 3 clip. Play a Grok clip.

**SPEAK**:
> "Photos don't just sit there. We animate them. VEO 3 from Google creates cinematic, lifelike motion. Grok Imagine gives you creative alternatives — and it's more flexible with content like family photos with kids."

**EDIT**:
- **Shots to capture**: 
  - Click "VEO 3" on a photo → show loading → play result (`/animations/pic1.mp4` or similar).
  - Click "Grok" on another photo → play result.
- **Files to prepare**: Use `public/animations/pic1.mp4` through `pic9.mp4` — these are your animated clips. `public/remento.mp4` may be a reference/demo.
- **Animation versions**: Show version switcher (VEO #1, Grok #2) if you have multiple versions per photo.

---

### [2:30–2:45] KEN BURNS & SCRAPBOOK

**VISUAL**: Scrapbook modal — book-style layout, tape, gentle Ken Burns. Or album preview with Ken Burns on photos.

**SPEAK**:
> "Not every photo needs AI animation. Ken Burns gives every image gentle motion — pan, zoom — for free. And if you don't want a full film, you can create an animated scrapbook. A digital keepsake that breathes with your stories."

**EDIT**: Open Scrapbook modal. Show page flip. Show Ken Burns on a photo in the timeline.

---

## 🎁 PART 6: EXPORT & GIFT (2:45–3:10)

### [2:45–3:00] EXPORT

**VISUAL**: "Generate Recap Video" button. Export progress. Finished video playing.

**SPEAK**:
> "When you're ready, we stitch everything together. Animated clips, AI narration, synced to each segment. You get a video you can download. Share. Give as a gift."

**EDIT**: Click export. Show progress bar. Play final video (with narration audio).

---

### [3:00–3:10] CLOSING HOOK

**VISUAL**: Film Ready badge. Family watching on screen. Or app logo.

**SPEAK**:
> "Living Memory. Your stories will live here forever. Preserve them while the people who lived them are still here."

**EDIT**: End on logo or warm family moment. Fade to black. Optional: "Built with Gemini 3" title card.

## 🛠️ ANIMATIONS TO EDIT / PREPARE

### Existing Assets

| Path | Use |
|------|-----|
| `public/animations/pic1.mp4` … `pic9.mp4` | VEO/Grok demo clips — use best 2–3 for video |
| `public/remento.mp4` | Possibly Remento reference or placeholder |
| `public/pic1.PNG` … `pic9.PNG` | Intro background, B-roll |

### Shots to Capture in-App

1. **Intro**: Full EVA intro flow — orb appears, typewriter syncs with voice. Let it run 30–45 seconds.
2. **Capture**: Camera → scan → photo extracted. Then: gallery upload.
3. **Conversation**: Type or speak a short story. Show EVA response.
4. **Album editor**: Drag photo to timeline. Open narration tab. Edit a segment.
5. **Animation**: Click VEO 3, show loading, play result. Same for Grok.
6. **Scrapbook**: Open modal, flip a page.
7. **Export**: Click Generate Recap Video, show progress, play output.

### Post-Production Notes

- **Music**: Soft, hopeful, ambient. Avoid dramatic or corporate.
- **Voiceover**: Warm, conversational. Not salesy.
- **Pacing**: Start strong, slow when showing conversations, brisk for features.
- **Text overlays**: Use sparingly — "EVA"., "VEO 3", "Grok Imagine", "Living Memory".
- **Resolution**: 1080p minimum. 4K if exporting for high-quality share.
